💡**Red** = Start w/ Action Verbs; if it’s in a current role, you can put as “present” and if past, you put as -Ed (Example of great action verbs: Collaborated, managed, improved, analyzed)

💡**Orange** = Hard Skills: I put specific hard skills & tools in my resume to show that I fit the job description's qualifications; at the bottom, I have the skills and match to my experiences where applicable. (Example: I went for Ops roles so I put skills from industry I learned from my research/networking with others in role)

💡Not colored, but at top = Education: put where I graduated, GPA if over a 3.5, organizations/awards, etc. IMO Relevant coursework is optional; add this if you don't have relevant experience (yet)

Header: Location as (City, State), LinkedIn (linked), phone number, and email (Optional: Portfolio/Personal Website); Education = top if student/new grad

💡Green = impact metrics: numbers and percentages from positions I worked in and showcase what I did to impact the company. I get these numbers through a) asking my peers/managers b) calculated estimations

💡Light blue = Impact statements before number/percentages: shows if I improved, optimized, or increased xyz which is related to Operations lingo

💡Yellow = soft skills: simple statements, these are collaboration, providing recommendations, and identifying solutions to problems

Note:

1) I use Google’s XYZ formula: Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z].

2) I came from a “non-target school” where many of my opportunities could not come from simply applying online/getting interviews

3) I landed my first role at Snap by using LinkedIn to identify the recruiter + showcased why I was a great candidate
